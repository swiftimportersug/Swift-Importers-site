Return & Refund Policy
🛡️

Return & Refund Policy

Thank you for shopping with Swift Importers Uganda. Please read carefully before completing your purchase.

1
General Return Conditions

We offer a 5-day return window from date of delivery. Item must be unused, in original packaging, with proof of purchase.

2
Defective Items (Dead on Arrival)

Notify us within 48 hours of receiving. We test the item at our workshop. If a genuine factory defect is confirmed, we offer a replacement or store credit/refund. Issues from power surges or physical damage do not qualify.

3
Non-Returnable Items

We do not accept returns for change of mind, items with missing serial numbers, or items damaged by liquid spills or physical force.

4
Inspection & Anti-Fraud

All premium electronics are logged with Serial Numbers prior to delivery. No refund is processed until a 24-48 hour technical inspection verifies the returned unit.

5
Return Shipping

Except for verified defective items, the customer covers all transport costs for returning the item to our Kampala office.

6
Refunds & Store Credit

Approved refunds processed via Mobile Money or Bank Transfer within 3-5 business days. A 20% restocking fee may apply for opened but undamaged packaging.

Payment Guide
💰

Simple & Flexible Payments

At Swift Importers Uganda, we have made payments as easy and flexible as possible.

1
Mobile Money - Kampala Residents

Pay via MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money to any of our provided numbers. Send us a screenshot on WhatsApp and we process your order immediately. You can pay before or on delivery.

2
Cash on Delivery - Kampala & Suburbs

Pay cash to our delivery rider on arrival. For orders above UGX 350,000, we require Mobile Money payment. You may also split payment - part by Mobile Money and remainder in cash as long as cash portion does not exceed UGX 350,000.

3
Outside Kampala - Full Mobile Money

Full payment via Mobile Money is required before items are dispatched via taxi or bus. Cash on delivery is only available within our direct delivery network. Bus and taxi transport fees range from UGX 5,000 to UGX 20,000 depending on item size.
